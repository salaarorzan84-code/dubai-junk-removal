How to Get Rid of Old Furniture in Dubai

Published 2026-10-26

Old sofas, worn-out beds and unused wardrobes take up space, and getting rid of them in Dubai is not always obvious, especially when you are moving out or renovating. Here are the main options, and how to choose the right one.

1. Sell it or give it away

If your furniture is still in good condition, someone may want it. Post clear photos on UAE classifieds apps and websites such as Dubizzle, in neighbourhood and community groups, or on your building's notice board. Being honest about the condition and offering free pickup usually gets a quick reply.

This works best for sofas, dining sets, beds and wardrobes that are clean and in working shape. It takes time though, so it is not ideal if you are moving out soon.

2. Donate it

Some charities and community organisations accept furniture in good condition. Ask whether they collect from your area and what they accept before you plan anything, as many will not take damaged, stained or very old items.

3. Check with your building management

Before moving anything out, ask your building management or landlord about their rules. Many buildings need you to book the service lift, restrict moving hours or have their own process for bulky items. Knowing this first avoids delays and problems on the day.

4. Hire a furniture removal service

If the furniture is broken, bulky, or you simply do not have the time, a furniture removal service is the easiest option. A team comes to your home or office, carries the items out and takes them away, so you do not have to arrange transport or disposal yourself.

This is also the practical choice when you are moving out, clearing a villa, or replacing a lot of furniture at once.

What not to do

Do not leave furniture beside bins, in corridors or in common areas of your building. It is usually not allowed and can cause problems with neighbours and building management.

How to prepare for furniture removal

  • Make a list of everything that needs to go (sofas, beds, mattresses, wardrobes, tables, appliances).
  • Take clear photos, including the items and the access route (floor, lift, stairs, parking).
  • Empty drawers, cupboards and wardrobes, and remove personal belongings.
  • Book the service lift or loading time with your building if needed.
  • Tell the team about narrow stairs, tight corners or items that need to be taken apart.

How much does furniture removal cost in Dubai?

There is no single price, because every job is different. The cost mainly depends on how much needs to be removed, the type and size of the items, the floor and whether there is a lift, parking and access, and the location in Dubai.

The easiest way to get an accurate price is to send photos on WhatsApp and ask for a quote before the job.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I leave old furniture next to the bins?

It is usually not allowed and can cause problems with your building management or the community, so it is better to arrange a proper removal.

Yes, our team carries items down for you. Tell us the floor and whether there is a lift so we can plan the job.
